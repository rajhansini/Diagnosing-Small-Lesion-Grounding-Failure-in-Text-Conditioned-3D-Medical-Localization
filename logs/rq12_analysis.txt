RQ12: does the smaller window improve GROUNDING, or only overlap?

[A] THE TIE ARTIFACT: how many voxels share the maximum heatmap value?
    A sliding window of stride s makes the heatmap piecewise-constant over s^3 blocks, so
    'the peak voxel' is ambiguous and np.argmax silently returns the block's corner.
window   stride     median ties   block s^3
32       16                4096        4096
8        8                  512         512

    The median tie count equals the stride block exactly, confirming the mechanism.
    RQ11 (Section 6.3) used the first-argmax rule at stride 16, so its pointing hit rates
    are biased LOW and its peak-to-lesion distances biased HIGH. Corrected below.
