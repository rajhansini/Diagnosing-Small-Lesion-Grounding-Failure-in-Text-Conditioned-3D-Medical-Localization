# `figures/` — generated report figures

All 29 images here are regenerated from the per-patient CSVs in [`../results/`](../results/) and the
training logs in [`../logs/`](../logs/) by one of twelve figure scripts in [`../src/`](../src/). None
was drawn or edited by hand, so a figure and the corresponding table in the report cannot drift apart.

Colours follow the Okabe-Ito colourblind-safe palette throughout, verified computationally (all pairs
clear ΔE ≥ 8 under simulated protanopia and deuteranopia).

## Caveat when reading the Otsu-scored comparison figures

`fig_leaderboard.png`, `fig4_scale_comparison.png`, `fig5_window_curve.png`,
`fig_significance_heatmap.png` and the right-hand panel of `fig_accuracy_vs_localization.png` all plot
**Otsu-thresholded** Dice, the project's original protocol. Section 7.11 shows Otsu *understates* the
smaller-window benefit by 2-3× relative to calibrated rules, and that `fig5_window_curve.png`'s two
smallest points (8³, 6³) also changed the tiling convention, so its right-hand tail is not a
window-size effect. `fig_rq12_window_curve.png` is the corrected version of that curve and should be
read alongside it; `fig_threshold_ladder.png` and `fig_rq13_arms.png` show the same comparisons under
every rule at once.
